# Mnemit audit of 10.1.2 and its fixes (30.10.2026)

The audit of 10.1.2 (253d2ce) under EF's full autonomy, and what 11.0 changed because of it. EF's brief: find every
rough edge a learner meets, look through the personas, change UX, logic, names and entities where the learner gains.
His four examples: the last card's empty outline before the celebration, Done as the default on "Ring closed", help
popovers that cut their text, a stepper over 36,500 days.

## 1. How it was found

| Source | What | Findings |
|---|---|---|
| Code, scheduling (`VocabCore` Scheduling, Session, Optimization) | FSRS-6 port, the 4:00 day, new cards, leeches, refit; probe tests | 5 S3 |
| Code, integrity (package, merge, undo, save, ledger, `deck-from-tsv`) | probe tests in a worktree | 4 S1, 4 S2, 3 S3 |
| Code, figures of the day (ring, streak, words, minutes, widget, reminders) | every surface against `DailyProgress.evaluate` | 4 S2, 8 S3 |
| Code, usability (scenarios S1–S7 of `quality-plan.md`, strings, states) | steps counted per scenario | 15 S3 |
| EF's own log (373 reviews, 21–30.10) | per day, per deck, recall against target, ledger against logs | 2 S2, 4 S3 |
| Screens, light | 426 frames at iPhone 13 mini size and 38 Mac frames from the snapshot tests | 4 S2, 40 S3, 55 S4 |
| Screens, dark | contrast measured on the frames | 5 S3, 8 S4 |

What EF's log showed: every learning day has reviews (the streak holds), a third of the reviews fall between 00:00 and
04:00, recall after a day or more is 82 % against the 90 % target on default FSRS weights, and no deck reached the 400
reviews a fit needs on its own.

## 2. What changed

### Data and integrity (the S1)

- Undo of a grade takes back only its own review: a review of the other device merged in after it stays
  (`Card.takeBack`/`putBack`). A taken-back review is kept as a note (`Card.undone`, a line `"undone":true` in
  `reviews.jsonl`), so a merge with a copy that saw the grade keeps it out. Deck format version 7: an older app would read
  that line as a review, so it rejects the deck. Both devices and `deck-from-tsv` (`swift run` rebuilds it) go to 11.0
  together.
- A `reviews.jsonl` torn short of a card's last review fails to open with its own message instead of being saved short.
- A failed save is shown ("Something is wrong with this deck"), retried every 30 s, and a deck that can't save stays
  open (the Mac doesn't quit while one fails). An unreadable conflict version is copied to `.backups` and resolved.
- The progress ledger is written only from complete data: not while a deck file failed to read, never over an
  unreadable own file. Records carry the rule that wrote them; EF's records of 21–25.10, which counted Greek B1 twice
  (the Mac passed one deck as a document and as a file), are rebuilt from the logs on the first run. The ring now drops a
  deck passed twice, and the Mac compares deck URLs through symlinks.
- The review-log encoder compares the whole log of a card, so a duration a merge fills in is saved.

### The day's figures

- One rule for "Words learned" (a card that has left learning, relearning included) in the headline, the day's gain
  and the ledger: it never falls overnight.
- "Rings closed" means the same on Progress and in the month's recap (`DayRecord.closedWithWork`).
- A day whose plan is empty only because every deck is paused or every card suspended is idle: the streak neither grows
  nor breaks, no medal comes of it. A deck with nothing due still keeps the streak.
- Every minute figure of a day uses the pace the day began with.
- The widget after 4:00 shows the day's goal from the day's time and the streak step's price; with no deck it writes
  no snapshot. The reminder names the minutes of the day's first two minutes of cards and a streak that will still hold.
- The study day follows a time-zone change; the new-card allowances are kept per study day.

### Scheduling and the memory model

- The memory model belongs to the learner: the automatic refit fits on the reviews of all decks of the folder and gives
  the parameters to every deck but those set by hand (an Anki preset). "Usable reviews N / 400" counts all decks. A fit
  is kept only when it beats the noise of the holdout by 1.5 standard errors.
- Every Forgot counts toward a leech except the first answer of a new card (EF's log had cards forgotten 6–16 times with
  3 lapses). Threshold 8; the moment fires at the next checkpoint, so old cards don't stop the first stream.
- A paused deck opened by itself with New cards/day Auto plans no new card.
- A live review's fuzz uses the replay's seed, so a merge gives the same due date.
- New cards/day can be None (0).

### Screens

- "Catch Up" is "Keep Going" everywhere; on "Ring closed" it is the default and "Done for Now" the second button. At the
  plan's end Done stays the default. No secondary button is grey: they are accent-tinted.
- The ring sets off with the last card, so no empty outline waits for it.
- Deck options are menus, not steppers: New cards/day (Auto, None, 5–100), Maximum reviews/day (50–500, No limit),
  Leech threshold (Off, 4–20), Learning steps (1–5), Maximum interval (half a year to 10 years, No limit for 36,500 days);
  an off-menu value stays on its menu. Help texts are at most about twenty words, in a popover of fixed width that
  scrolls past 320 pt. The FSRS header reads "Memory Model (FSRS)".
- Today: an unreadable deck no longer holds every figure back, its row says why; a lost iCloud folder is kept and
  reported with Choose Folder Again; figures of an ended day are held back; a streak that ended is told once; a reminder
  tap opens the stream as the widget does. Failure screens offer Try Again; a deck downloading says so. The introduction
  can be skipped from its first screen.
- Add Cards counts words on both apps, opens with the keyboard in list mode and names the deck; Edit Card from the
  stream opens in the answer; ⌘E is in the cheat sheet. The import preview's numbers add up. A–Z in the card browser
  sorts by the word without its article. New Deck needs a free, non-empty name.
- The study card at accessibility sizes keeps its buttons and text in view (the card's text is capped at AX3); a Greek
  headword keeps its endings ("-η, -ο") on its line.
- Dark mode: text in the accent uses a lighter indigo (`Color.appAccentText`, 4.5:1 or more on every surface), labels on
  filled buttons are white, a missed day shows as a grey ring, locked medals and the ring's track are visible, the
  maturity ramp's lowest stage stands off the card.

## 3. Decisions taken and how to undo them

| Decision | Why | Rollback |
|---|---|---|
| "Keep Going" for the moment after the ring | EF: "Catch Up" reads as falling behind; the learner just goes on | strings in `TodaySummary`, `StreamRestView`, `NextStep` |
| Format 7 for the undo note | an older app would count an undone review | revert P1's first commit; decks written by 11.0 then need a 10.x that reads 7 |
| Pooled refit, 1.5 SE margin | per deck none of EF's decks reaches 400; at 400 reviews a fit is mostly noise | `ParameterRefit.requiredStandardErrors`; pooling in `ReviewPool` |
| Leech counts every Forgot | a word forgotten 16 times is a leech for any learner | `Card.forgotCount` |
| Idle day for an all-paused plan | a holiday pause must not grow a streak | `DailyProgress.evaluate` |
| Card text capped at AX3 | at AX5 the card's text cut its answer | one `.dynamicTypeSize` line in `CardFace` |
| Russian in «ты» | the app's voice | the catalog |
| "Hint" stays the name of the question's note | renaming the field across the product costs more than it gives | — |

## 4. Open

- Not proven by reading: whether NSDocument and UIDocument write the package atomically when the app is killed mid-save
  (kill test), and whether `deck-from-tsv` loses a grade made in the 5 s before it runs while the Mac app holds the deck.
- A card's due date is an absolute instant: after travel across many zones it is due a few hours off its local day.
- Per-deck bookmarks that fail to resolve are still dropped. `OpenDeck.close()` returning false is ignored by
  `StudyRun.close`, `Shell.forget`, `DeckRefits` and the iPhone's `previous.close()`.
- Polish left (S4): `references/` keeps the list in the fixers' notes of this audit; none blocks a learner.
- To see on the devices: §5.
